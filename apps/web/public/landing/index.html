What We Do

Our Services

From concept to installation, we guide each project with meticulous attention to seasonal rhythm and spatial harmony.

Floral Design

Bespoke arrangements for ceremonies, events, and editorial shoots. Each composition is seasonally led and site-specific.

  • Wedding & ceremony florals
  • Editorial & campaign styling
  • Corporate & hospitality
  • Private commissions

Spatial Installation

Large-scale botanical installations that transform galleries, retail spaces, and private residences into immersive environments.

  • Gallery & museum installations
  • Retail window & interior
  • Pop-up & event activation
  • Residential styling

Creative Direction

Full-service creative direction for brands seeking a botanical aesthetic — from mood boards to final still life photography.

  • Brand campaign concepts
  • Still life & product styling
  • Art direction for print
  • Lookbook & catalogue

Sourcing & Consulting

Rare and seasonal flower sourcing for discerning clients. We maintain direct relationships with growers across Europe and Japan.

  • Seasonal sourcing calendars
  • Import & logistics
  • Studio setup consulting
  • Team training
